# Byte Bistro Employee Handbook

Byte Bistro is a small coffee shop and coworking cafe in Austin, Texas, founded in 2021 by Maria Okafor.

Opening hours are 7am to 9pm Monday to Friday, and 8am to 6pm on weekends. The cafe is closed on Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

Every employee gets one free drink per shift and a 40% discount on food. Discounts do not apply to retail coffee beans.

The Wi-Fi network is called ByteGuest and the password changes every Monday. Staff can find the current password on the whiteboard behind the espresso machine.

Shift swaps must be posted in the #shifts Slack channel at least 48 hours before the shift. A manager has to approve every swap with a thumbs up emoji.

If the espresso machine breaks, do not try to fix it yourself. Call our repair partner, Hank's Espresso Repair, at 512-555-0199. Hank usually arrives within two hours.

Employees accrue 1 hour of paid time off for every 25 hours worked. PTO requests go to the shift manager at least two weeks in advance.

The signature drink is the Stack Overflow Latte: a double shot of espresso, oat milk, maple syrup, and a pinch of sea salt. It costs $6.50.

Our dog policy: dogs are welcome on the patio but not inside, except for service animals. Keep a bowl of water by the patio door at all times.

The closing shift must count the register, wipe down every table, empty the grinder, and lock the back door. The last person out sets the alarm with code 4417.
